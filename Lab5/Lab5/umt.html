About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a prominent public research university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Originally starting as a fisheries and marine science center under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in 1979, it underwent several developmental stages before officially attaining full university status in 2007. Guided by its motto "Ocean of Discoveries for Global Sustainability," UMT has grown into a specialized institution renowned for its heavy emphasis on marine science, maritime technology, aquatic resources, and environmental studies.

Situated along the scenic East Coast of Peninsular Malaysia near the South China Sea, the university's vibrant coastal campus provides an ideal setting for hands-on research and field-based learning. UMT features state-of-the-art facilities—including research vessels and marine stations—supporting a diverse array of undergraduate and postgraduate programs that extend beyond marine biology into computing, maritime management, and agrotechnology. Through its commitment to academic excellence and environmental stewardship, UMT continues to produce skilled graduates while driving critical research for regional and global sustainability.
